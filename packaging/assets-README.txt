OPTIONAL SOUND PACK
===================

Chakravyuha creates all of its music and sound effects itself, so this
folder can stay empty. If you put audio files here, they replace the
built-in sounds one by one (.wav, .ogg, .mp3 or .flac):

  assets\music\menu.ogg        music for the menus
  assets\music\battle.ogg      music during a battle

  assets\sfx\step              the Pandava's footstep
  assets\sfx\enemy_step        the Kaurava's armoured step
  assets\sfx\blocked           no path that way
  assets\sfx\clash             sword clash when breaching a gate
  assets\sfx\clash_heavy       the killing blow
  assets\sfx\drum_roll         the formation shifts
  assets\sfx\conch             the battle begins
  assets\sfx\victory           victory
  assets\sfx\defeat            defeat
  assets\sfx\capture           a gate is captured
  assets\sfx\enemy_gate        the Kaurava pushes through a gate
  assets\sfx\ui_move           moving through a menu
  assets\sfx\ui_select         choosing a menu item
  assets\sfx\dice              Shakuni's dice
  assets\sfx\danger            the heartbeat drum when the Kaurava is close

For example: assets\sfx\clash.wav

The title screen shows how many sound-pack files were found.
