CHAKRAVYUHA  v1.1.0
The spiral formation of Kurukshetra
==================================================================

STARTING THE GAME
  Double-click Chakravyuha.exe. There is nothing to install.

  Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game is not
  code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

  Needs: Windows 7 or later (64-bit) and graphics that support OpenGL 3.3.

THE GOAL
  A Pandava warrior must breach five rings of rooms and reach the sacred
  fire at the centre. A Kaurava warrior hunts them. Rings are joined only by
  one bronze gate each, and every few gate crossings the whole formation
  shifts: every gate jumps to a new room.

  Pandava wins by reaching the fire. Kaurava wins by landing on the
  Pandava's room.

GAME MODES
  Solo Battle       You lead one army; the computer leads the other.
                    (The Kaurava AI hunts with A* search; the Pandava AI
                    flees using breadth-first-search distance maps.)
  Two Warriors      Two players on one keyboard. Player 1 uses WASD,
                    Player 2 uses the arrow keys. Choose sides or let
                    Shakuni's dice decide.

  Before each battle you forge your warrior: type a name, pick a legend
  (Abhimanyu, Arjuna, Karna, Drona...) or design your own face.

CONTROLS
  A / D   or  Left / Right    walk around your ring
  W       or  Up              go through a gate, inward
  S       or  Down            fall back outward through a gate
  B    show your shortest route to the fire
  V    show the Kaurava's A* route
  L    room labels                 C   gate capture on/off
  [ ]  fewer/more crossings before the formation shifts
  P    autopilot (solo Pandava)    M   mute
  R    rematch   N  new battlefield   Esc  back / main menu
  The mouse works in every menu, and the buttons under the battlefield
  can be clicked.

SOUND PACK (optional)
  All music and sound is created by the game itself. To use your own
